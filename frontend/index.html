VerityAI

Evidence-grounded, multi-source claim checking

VerityAI does not know the truth of every statement. It retrieves evidence and reports what that evidence says — and what it does not.

Submit a claim

Submit one clear statement. Names, dates and numbers are kept as written.

Disclaimer. VerityAI is a research prototype. Model outputs can be wrong. Similarity, entailment and confidence scores describe an evidence–claim pair; they are not the probability that a claim is true. Always read the cited sources.

Sources queried: Wikipedia / MediaWiki, Wikidata, OpenAlex, and (when configured) ClaimReview fact-checks. Only permitted sources are queried.
